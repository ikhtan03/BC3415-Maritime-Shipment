# Controlled and Prohibited Goods for Imports
Understand the requirements for importing controlled goods and goods prohibited from importing to Singapore.
Last updated on 24 February 2026
Download the full version of the infographics [PDF, 3.00 MB] (opens in new tab) to view the linked resources.
## Controlled Goods
Controlled goods require proper authorisation (advance notification, licence or certificate approval) from Competent Authorities (CA) before they may be imported to Singapore. You are advised to check if the goods are controlled using the description of the goods, Harmonised System (HS) code or CA product code via the HS/CA Product Code Checker (opens in new tab). If the item is subject to control, you may check directly with the respective CAs on their licensing requirements. You may download the list of CAs' contact information via the HS/CA Product Code Checker.
## Prohibited Goods
The goods listed below cannot be imported into Singapore.
